📜 Terms & Policy

Please review before continuing to LockDoc

1. What LockDoc does

LockDoc lets you store documents in a private vault and share time-limited, read-only access to them via a scanned QR code. Shared views close automatically and are watermarked.

2. Your storage limit

Customer accounts include 3 MB of total document storage. Delete existing documents to free up space if you reach this limit. Administrator accounts are not subject to this limit.

3. What we store

We store the documents you upload, your account details, and a record of access sessions (who viewed what, and when) so both sides have a record that a verification took place.

4. What we don't do

We don't sell your documents or data. We don't track your live location. A viewing session shows the document only for its countdown window, then closes automatically.

5. Your responsibility

You're responsible for the accuracy of documents you upload and for only sharing vault QR codes with people you intend to grant access to.

6. Changes

These terms may be updated as the app evolves. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised terms.
